Chapter Five · A Letter

From your little brother.

Dear Groote Man,

Thirty. I keep saying it out loud and it still doesn't sound big enough for everything you've already done and everyone you've already been: the protector, the comedian, the standard, the softest heart in the loudest room.

You taught me things you don't even remember teaching me. How to laugh at the bad days until they behave. How to show up for people like it's a full-time job. How to walk into any room like the invitation was a formality.

People talk about dream girls like they're fiction. I grew up with one telling me to do my homework and stop touching her stuff.

So here's to your thirties: may they be softer than your twenties, louder in the ways that matter, quieter in the ways that don't, and absolutely filled with sunflowers. You deserve every single golden thing coming your way.

I'm proud to be your big brother. I always will be.

With all my love, Helao. ISJA SHO DA 30 YEARS
